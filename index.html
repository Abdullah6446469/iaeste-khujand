MEET IAESTE KHUJAND

Local community.
Global possibility.

We’re the Khujand Local Committee of IAESTE Tajikistan, connecting students in northern Tajikistan with the world of international practical experience.

Through IAESTE, students can build professional skills, experience another culture and form connections that last beyond an internship. We help you find the right place to begin.
