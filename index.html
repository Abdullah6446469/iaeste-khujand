We’re IAESTE Khujand. Your local connection to a world of practical experience.

As the Khujand Local Committee of IAESTE Tajikistan, we help students in northern Tajikistan find their starting point in the international IAESTE community.

It begins with professional experience. It grows through cultural exchange, friendship and the confidence to try something new.
