01 / WHO WE ARE

Local roots.
Global connections.

IAESTE is an international network that helps students gain practical work experience across borders. IAESTE Khujand is the local committee in northern Tajikistan, connected to the IAESTE Tajikistan National Committee.

We share a simple belief: professional growth and cultural understanding are stronger when people learn from one another, in new places and real workplaces.

A CITY OF EXCHANGE

03 / OUR CITY

A warm welcome
to Khujand.

Set beside the Syr Darya in northern Tajikistan, Khujand offers arriving interns a place to work, meet people and experience local life. The lively Panjshanbe Bazaar is one glimpse of the city’s everyday energy.

IAESTE’s exchange is about more than the workplace. It is also a chance to share perspectives and build friendships across cultures.
