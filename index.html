Ask IAESTE Khujand
Who can apply for an IAESTE internship?+

IAESTE offers experience in technical and natural science fields. Each placement has its own study, skills and language requirements. Contact IAESTE Khujand to confirm your eligibility and the national application process.

Are the internships paid?+

IAESTE Tajikistan describes on-site internships as paid placements. The amount and conditions appear in the individual offer. Remote internship conditions may differ, so check the offer before applying.

When should I start preparing?+

For summer 2027 internships, send your CV in English to iaeste@inbox.ru by 31 October 2026. A good command of English or German is required. See the application notice and confirm the next selection steps with the committee.

Which documents do I need?+

The national nomination checklist includes a CV, motivation letter, student forms, certificates and passport copies. See all twelve documents and official forms. The enrolment form linked for Germany is destination-specific.

Can I get international experience from home?+

Yes. IAESTE lists remote internships for students who want to collaborate with international organisations without travelling. Review the working hours, payment and technical requirements in each offer.

I’m coming to Khujand. Who should I contact?+

Call the Khujand committee on +992 92 773 50 05. Before arriving, confirm your placement arrangements and ask about the current cultural programme. The programme linked on this page is the 2024 archive.
